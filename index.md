<section class="section-shell">
  <p class="section-kicker">How I work</p>
  <h2 class="section-title">Prediction is a computational result; geological interpretation is a scientific argument.</h2>
  <div class="card-grid">
    <article class="domain-card"><span class="card-label">01</span><h3>Data authority first</h3><p>Provenance, QA/QC, exclusions, analytical comparability, and geological labels are defined before modelling.</p></article>
    <article class="domain-card"><span class="card-label">02</span><h3>Validate at the right geological scale</h3><p>Grouped, deposit-aware, spatial, geological, external, or prospective validation is preferred where random row splitting would leak context.</p></article>
    <article class="domain-card"><span class="card-label">03</span><h3>Represent uncertainty</h3><p>Threshold sensitivity, cohort choices, spatial uncertainty, transferability, and failure modes are treated as part of the result.</p></article>
    <article class="domain-card"><span class="card-label">04</span><h3>Separate prediction from mechanism</h3><p>Feature importance and high model accuracy can support investigation, but do not by themselves establish geological causality.</p></article>
  </div>
</section>
