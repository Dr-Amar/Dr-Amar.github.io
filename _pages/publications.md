# Publications

My publication record spans **mineral geochemistry, economic geology, GeoAI, sedimentary geochemistry, tectonics, and subsurface geoscience**. This page highlights outputs most relevant to my current research profile and links papers to public research artifacts where available.

For the complete and most current citation record, use **[Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=EDfhb1gAAAAJ&hl=en)**.

## GeoAI & mineral-exploration research

### Artificial intelligence-driven metallogenic typing of pyrite from global ore systems

**Journal:** *Journal of Geochemical Exploration* · Volume 289 (October 2026) · Article 108138  
**Status:** **Published / version of record** · available online 9 June 2026  
**Research focus:** global multielement pyrite geochemistry, metallogenic discrimination, class imbalance, blind testing, deposit-scale LOGO validation, and interpretable machine learning.

[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.gexplo.2026.108138) · [Public research repository](https://github.com/Dr-Amar/Pyrite-AI-metallogenic-typing)

### Big Data Mining on Galena Geochemistry Using Machine Learning Algorithms: Implications for Metallogenic Discrimination

**Journal:** *Mathematical Geosciences*  
**Status:** **Published online / Early Access**, 4 April 2026  
**Research focus:** galena trace-element geochemistry from 37 Pb-Zn deposits, metallogenic classification, RF/GB/MLP/SVM comparison, imbalance treatment, feature importance, and high-dimensional visualization.

[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11004-026-10274-0) · [Public research repository](https://github.com/Dr-Amar/Galena-Geochemistry-ML-Metallogenic-Discrimination)

### Ore genesis and critical metal enrichment using deep learning algorithms in Gunga Pb-Zn deposit, Southern Pakistan: Constraints from geochemistry and isotopic compositions

**Journal:** *Journal of Geochemical Exploration* (2025)  
**Status:** **Published / version of record**  
**Research focus:** sphalerite geochemistry, S-Pb isotopes, deep-learning classification, ore-genesis interpretation, and critical-metal enrichment including Ge-bearing sphalerite.

[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.gexplo.2025.107771) · [Public research repository](https://github.com/Dr-Amar/Sphalerite-Gunga-Pb-Zn-DeepLearning)

### Machine Learning-Driven Classification of Pb-Zn Ore Deposits Using Pyrite Trace Elements and Isotopic Signatures: A Case Study of the Gunga Deposit

**Journal:** *Journal of Geochemical Exploration* (2025)  
**Status:** **Published / version of record**  
**Research focus:** pyrite trace elements, isotopic signatures, global comparison data, ML classification, deposit-aware validation, and geological interpretation of the Gunga Pb-Zn system.

[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.gexplo.2025.107693) · [Public research repository](https://github.com/Dr-Amar/Pyrite-Gunga-Pb-Zn-Deposit--Machine-Learning)
